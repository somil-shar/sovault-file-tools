Everything You Need to Know About SoVault Free PDF Tools

Learn how our 100% private in-browser document processing engine works.

What makes SoVault the best free online PDF tool?

Unlike conventional web tools (such as iLovePDF or Smallpdf) that transmit your confidential files over the internet to cloud servers, SoVault runs 100% inside your web browser using HTML5 Canvas and WebAssembly. Your files are never uploaded, stored, or viewed by anyone.

How do I merge multiple PDF files together?

Select the PDF Merger tool, drag & drop your PDF documents into the upload area, arrange the page order as needed, and click "Merge & Download". The unified file is compiled instantly on your CPU.

Is there any file size limit or watermarks?

Zero limits and zero watermarks. Since processing is executed on your local machine, you can process large PDFs and high-resolution images freely without registration, subscriptions, or credit card requirements.

What PDF tools are included in SoVault?

SoVault includes 17 professional utilities: PDF Merger, PDF Splitter, PDF Compressor, PDF Rotator, Page Extractor, Text Extractor, PDF Metadata Editor, Image Compressor, Image Resizer, Format Converter, Image Cropper, PDF to JPG, Images to PDF, Screenshot to PDF, Images to ZIP, PDF to ZIP, and Print to PDF.
